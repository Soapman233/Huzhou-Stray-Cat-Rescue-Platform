## 四、长版仓库简介

用于 Gitee 仓库说明、掘金/知乎文章开头、个人主页项目卡片、答辩 PPT 项目简介页。

### 中文

> **HZSCA（Huzhou Stray Cat Association）** 是一个面向流浪猫救助公益组织的综合管理平台，采用「一套后端、两个前端」架构：微信小程序承载 C 端用户体验，Vue 3 管理后台承载 B 端运营工作台。
>
> 平台围绕救助业务的真实链路，解决三件事：**救助侧**支持志愿者与市民上报流浪猫线索（含定位、紧急度、图片）并由管理员统一调度；**领养侧**在用户填写偏好问卷后，通过**六维加权匹配算法**推荐适配猫咪，并基于问卷生成**领养风险评分**辅助审核决策；**运营侧**提供猫咪档案、特征标签、活动、志愿者审核、领养审核、救助工单与数据导出等完整能力。
>
> 区别于常见的「信息展示 + 增删改查」类项目，本项目把**推荐算法**与**风险评估**真正引入业务决策，并补齐了工程化能力：Docker 全栈一键部署、数据库启动自举、支付宝沙箱支付闭环（含每日对账与审计日志）、41 个覆盖边界的单元测试，以及前端分包优化（入口 chunk 1,141 KB → 25 KB）。

### English

> **HZSCA (Huzhou Stray Cat Association)** is a management platform for stray-cat rescue organizations, built as one backend with two frontends: a WeChat Mini Program for end users and a Vue 3 admin console for operations.
>
> It follows the real rescue workflow and addresses three concerns. **Rescue**: volunteers and citizens report stray-cat sightings — with location, urgency, and photos — for admins to dispatch and follow up. **Adoption**: after a user fills in a preference profile, a **six-dimension weighted matching model** recommends suitable cats, while a questionnaire-based **risk score** supports review decisions. **Operations**: cat records, trait tags, events, volunteer review, adoption review, rescue tickets, and data export.
>
> Unlike typical CRUD-style projects, HZSCA puts **recommendation** and **risk assessment** at the center of real decisions, and backs them with production-minded engineering: a one-command Docker stack, a self-bootstrapping database, a complete Alipay sandbox payment loop with daily reconciliation and audit logging, 41 boundary-focused unit tests, and frontend code-splitting that cut the entry chunk from 1,141 KB to 25 KB.

My undergraduate capstone project: one Spring Boot 3 backend, two frontends (a WeChat Mini Program and a Vue 3 admin console).

A few things I found worth building:
- Six-dimension weighted matching: hard exclusions (allergy × shedding, kids × not child-friendly, ...) followed by scoring across activity, affection, shedding, space, beginner-friendliness, and schedule

- Real reconciliation fallback: async notifications are assumed lossy, so orders are backfilled via active query, auto-closed on timeout, and audited daily
